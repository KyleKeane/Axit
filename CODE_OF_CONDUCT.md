# Code of conduct

Axit is made for and with people who use screen readers. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere the project is represented, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all;
- assume good faith, and criticise ideas and code, never people;
- welcome people of every background, identity and ability, and write so that a screen reader reads it well (plain
  text, no information only in images or colour);
- keep other people's personal data private, theirs and the project's (`PRIVACY.md`).

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or attention, threats, publishing someone's
private information, and deliberately disrupting the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
(<https://github.com/KyleKeane/Axit/security/advisories/new>), marking it as a conduct report, or through the contact
on www.kylekeane.com. Reports are read in confidence, and the reporter is never exposed.

## What happens

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone whose behaviour does. The decision is explained to the
person concerned.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).
