# axClaude: a set of two questions (docs/axclaude/nvda-test-plan.md section 12 step 19). The first question reads
# itself; after its answer the second cuts in and reads itself, with nothing of the first read again.
mkdir q
start claude {temp}\q -- --model haiku
try said trust
iffound wait 1500
iffound keys enter
expect said Ready
type Use your AskUserQuestion tool once, with two questions: which colour I prefer, red or blue, and which season I prefer, summer or winter.
keys enter
expect said ! Question 1 of 2
wait 1500
keys enter
expect said ! Question 2 of 2
said
wait 1500
keys enter
said
# After the last answer the app submits Claude's review itself, and the message field comes back (the set's last
# question stayed in its place until 2026-10-23 and took the next message as its answer).
expect focus Message to Claude
